# 🛡️ Incident Response Playbook

## 1. Detection

What triggered the investigation?

## 2. Triage

Determine:

- scope;
- affected assets;
- users;
- severity;
- indicators.

## 3. Investigation

Collect and correlate available telemetry.

## 4. Containment

Prevent further activity.

## 5. Eradication

Remove the cause of the incident.

## 6. Recovery

Restore normal operations.

## 7. Validation

Confirm that the threat has been removed.

## 8. Lessons Learned

Identify improvements.

## 9. Detection Improvements

Update or create detections based on the investigation.
